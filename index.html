Introduction

Welcome to Color Rush, a fast-paced offline reflex game developed and published by Shiva Gaming Studio ("we," "our," or "us"). We are based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, and we are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how our application behaves on your device.

This Privacy Policy describes the data practices for the Color Rush mobile application (the "App" or "Game") available on Google Play. By downloading or using Color Rush, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This document is written in plain language so that all players — including parents and guardians reviewing it on behalf of younger players — can clearly understand what data is and is not collected.

💡

The Short Version Color Rush does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or our servers. The only data that exists lives locally on your own device.

03

Offline Gameplay & Local Storage

Color Rush follows an Offline-First architecture. Every core gameplay feature is fully functional without an internet connection.

  • 🔌
    No Internet Required

    Color Rush runs entirely on-device. You can play in airplane mode, on a remote island, or underground with full functionality.

  • 🚫
    No Login or Registration

    The App has no sign-in screen, no user account, no guest session, and no profile of any kind. You simply install and play.

  • 💾
    Local Progress Storage

    Your best score, earned stars, daily streak counter, unlocked colour collections, and preferences (music, sound effects, haptics) are stored in your device's private app sandbox using SharedPreferences. We cannot access this data.

  • 🗑️
    Data Deletion

    Uninstalling the App or clearing its local storage permanently deletes all locally stored data. Because no data lives on our servers, we are unable to recover it for you.

  • 🔒
    No Server Communication

    Color Rush makes zero API calls to any server operated by us. There is no backend, no database, and no cloud synchronisation of any kind.

04

Advertising & Monetisation (Google AdMob)

Color Rush is a free-to-play game with no in-app purchases. To sustain development, we integrate Google AdMob, a third-party advertising platform operated by Google LLC. AdMob may display advertisements within the App and may independently collect certain device-level information to serve those ads. The App itself does not collect personal information.

Types of Advertisements in Color Rush

⭐
Rewarded Ads

Optional. You choose to watch a short video in exchange for in-game stars, a free continue after a Game Over, or a Checkpoint Resume from the Home Screen. Watching is never forced.

📺
Interstitial Ads

Full-screen ads shown at natural transition points — at most once every three Game Overs. Never shown during active gameplay.

🚫
No Banner Ads

We have made a deliberate choice not to show persistent banner ads. Gameplay is never obstructed by an always-visible advertisement.

What AdMob May Automatically Collect

When ads load, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process the following data independently to deliver, personalise, and improve advertising. This data is collected by Google — not by us:

  • Advertising ID (GAID) — your device's resettable ad identifier
  • Device Information — device model, OS version, screen resolution, and language
  • IP Address — used to infer approximate geographic region (not precise GPS)
  • Diagnostics — app performance data and ad loading diagnostics
  • Crash Information — crash reports related to ad rendering, sent to Google
  • Ad Interaction Events — impressions, clicks, and video completion signals
📡

Offline Mode — No Ads, No AdMob Data When you play Color Rush without an internet connection, no ad requests are made. AdMob does not collect any data when the device is offline. The game automatically provides rewards so your experience is uninterrupted.

Google AdMob operates under Google's own Privacy Policy. We strongly encourage you to review it: policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device's Google Settings → Ads menu or by resetting your Advertising ID.

Data Security

The most secure data is data that is never collected in the first place. Color Rush's architecture reflects this principle.

  • 🛡️
    No Server-Side Risk

    Because we do not operate a backend database or user account system, there is no central repository of your information that could be breached, leaked, or accessed by an unauthorised party.

  • 📱
    Device-Level Protection

    Local game data stored in SharedPreferences is protected by your Android device's application sandbox. Other apps on your device cannot access Color Rush's private storage. Your device's lock screen, encryption, and security settings provide the relevant protections.

  • 🔐
    Third-Party Security

    Google AdMob and Google Play Services are operated by Google LLC, which maintains industry-leading security standards including ISO/IEC 27001 certification, SOC 2 compliance, and end-to-end encryption. Their security practices are described in Google's own documentation.

ℹ️

Our Commitment While no digital system is completely immune to risk, the zero-collection design of Color Rush means that even in the most adverse hypothetical scenarios, there is no personal data of yours stored anywhere that we could compromise.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain legal rights regarding your personal data. Under frameworks such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), these rights typically include the right to access, correct, delete, and restrict processing of your data.

Because Color Rush does not collect any personal data from you directly, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, restrict, or delete on our end. The only data that exists about your gameplay lives on your own device and is entirely under your control.

What You Can Do

  • Delete local data: Uninstall the App or go to Android Settings → Apps → Color Rush → Clear Data.
  • Opt out of personalised ads: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Reset your Ad ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID.
  • Control Google's data: Visit myaccount.google.com to manage data Google holds about you.

If you have any concerns about your rights, please contact us using the information in Section 14 and we will do our best to assist you or point you to the appropriate party.
